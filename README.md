# B2B Sales Performance & Profitability Analytics

> **Power BI Business Intelligence Project | Sales, Profitability, Product Portfolio, Markets & Execution**

An end-to-end **Power BI dashboard** designed to analyze B2B sales performance, profitability, product portfolio health, regional performance, sales channels, customer segments, discounts, returns, and business-event impact.

The dashboard transforms transactional sales data into an executive-friendly analytical solution with interactive filters, KPI cards, drill-down analysis, and actionable business recommendations.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The dashboard was designed to answer key business questions:

- How is sales performance trending over time?
- Are we achieving our sales targets?
- Which regions contribute the most revenue?
- Which product categories drive revenue and profit?
- How do discounts affect profitability?
- Which products/categories have stronger margins?
- Which sales channels generate the most revenue?
- Where are return rates becoming a concern?
- Which customer segments have the highest value?

## 💡 Key Business Insights

### 📈 Sales Growth

The analysis shows strong overall sales growth, with a noticeable acceleration in the later period. However, sales growth needs to be evaluated together with return-rate and target-achievement trends.

### 📅 Seasonality

Sales show a recurring **Q4 peak**, indicating a meaningful seasonal pattern. This can support better inventory, staffing, and operational capacity planning.

### 💰 Product Profitability

Different product categories contribute differently to revenue and profitability. The analysis demonstrates why management should evaluate both **gross profit and gross margin**, rather than relying on revenue alone.

### 🏷️ Discount Impact

Higher discount levels can reduce profitability. The discount analysis helps identify areas where promotional strategies may need tighter controls.

### 🌍 Regional Performance

Regional target variance differs significantly across markets. This indicates that regional performance should be analyzed individually rather than relying only on company-level averages.

### 📦 Channel Performance

Sales channels differ in revenue contribution, margin, and return-rate behavior. This creates opportunities to optimize channel-specific sales and quality strategies.

### 👥 Customer Segments

Customer segments show different order volumes and average order values, suggesting that customer strategy can be tailored according to **volume vs. value** characteristics.

### ⚙️ Operational Events

Business-event analysis identifies periods where operational disruptions coincide with changes in sales, margins, and return rates.

---

## 🚀 Business Recommendations

Based on the dashboard analysis:

1. **Plan inventory and operational capacity around recurring seasonal demand.**

2. **Investigate increasing return rates** before focusing exclusively on additional sales growth.

3. **Strengthen software/product attach opportunities** where they can improve blended profitability.

4. **Introduce stronger controls around deep-discount promotions.**

5. **Review high-return sales channels and customer offers** to identify quality or expectation gaps.

6. **Use regional performance differences** to create targeted improvement plans.

7. **Analyze top salesperson practices** to identify repeatable approaches that can be shared with the wider sales team.

8. **Build contingency plans for operational disruptions** that materially affect returns and margins.
